A one-liner for the make.fun bounty "The Moon Finally Answers Back": a single
line, in the voice of the Moon replying at last to the wishes, howls and
late-night promises of everyone staring up at it.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`
when that variable is set). Standard library only, no network, no secrets.

## The line

> Four billion years of wishes, howls, and promises you'll change, and your best one is still 'please let him text back.' I move oceans. I can't move on from your ex for you.

172 characters, one line.
